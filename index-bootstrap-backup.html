About me

Engineer · Site · Design
SITE: NUAGAON, ODISHA

I'm a civil engineer who likes being where the concrete is poured. I joined JSW Steel as a Graduate Engineer Trainee in 2024 and was promoted to Assistant Manager – Civil within a year, based on performance and execution ownership.

My day-to-day sits where execution, quality and cost meet: coordinating mechanical and electrical interfaces for mill foundations, enforcing IS 456 and IS 10262 compliance, preparing QAPs and FQAPs, and certifying contractor bills and measurements. I build Excel dashboards for management and automate site documentation with Power Automate and SharePoint.

Outside the plant I design homes: plans, structural drawing sets, bar bending schedules and photo-real 3D walkthroughs in SketchUp, Twinmotion and Blender.
