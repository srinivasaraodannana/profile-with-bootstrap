01 / Profile Narrative

ENGINEER
in the making.

I'm Dannana Srinivasa Rao — a civil engineer who thrives on site where the concrete is poured. I joined JSW Steel Ltd. as a Graduate Engineer Trainee in 2024 and was fast-track promoted to Assistant Manager – Civil within one year, driven by execution ownership and cost optimization.

My operational day sits where engineering execution, quality assurance, and fiscal accountability meet: coordinating mechanical and electrical interfaces for heavy mill foundations (Ball Mills & Vertimills), enforcing strict IS 456 and IS 10262 standards, preparing QAPs & FQAPs, and certifying contractor measurements and bills for multi-crore industrial packages.

Beyond industrial scale, I design homes: architectural plans, complete structural drawing sets (footings, columns, plinth & roof beams, slabs, staircases), bar bending schedules (BBS), and photorealistic 3D walkthroughs crafted in SketchUp, Blender, and Twinmotion.

To streamline field workflows, I build custom Excel tracking dashboards and automate site photo documentation pipelines with Power Automate and SharePoint.
